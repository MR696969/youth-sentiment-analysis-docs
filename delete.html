Data Deletion Request

Last updated: March 2024

Your Right to Data Deletion

Under data protection regulations, you have the right to request the deletion of your data. We are committed to honoring these requests promptly and efficiently.

How to Request Data Deletion

  1. Send an email to maheswarrana49@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request"
  2. Include the following information in your email:
    • Your name
    • The social media platform(s) you used with our service
    • Any specific data you want deleted
  3. We will process your request within 30 days
  4. You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete

What We Delete

Upon request, we will delete:

  • Any stored API credentials
  • Analysis results associated with your account
  • Any cached data from your social media interactions

Important Notes

  • We do not store personal data beyond what is necessary for the service
  • Deletion requests are processed within 30 days
  • You may need to revoke API access from the respective social media platforms
